1 Purpose

1.1 This procedure describes how a branch opens a savings account for an individual customer.

2 Eligibility

2.1 Applicants must be 18 years of age or older.

2.2 Minors aged below 10 years are not eligible to open an account. Minors aged 10 years and above may open an account jointly with a guardian, as described in Section 9.

3 Know Your Customer (KYC)

3.1 The branch officer collects proof of identity and proof of address from the applicant.

3.2 If both documents are verified, the application proceeds to risk assessment.

3.3 If either document cannot be verified, the application is rejected.

4 Risk assessment

4.1 The branch officer assigns a risk rating of low, medium or high.

4.2 Low-risk applications are approved and the account is opened.

4.3 High-risk applications are referred to the Branch Manager.

4.4 The Branch Manager approves or rejects referred applications within 2 working days.

5 Account opening

5.1 The account must be opened within 3 working days of receiving a complete application.
