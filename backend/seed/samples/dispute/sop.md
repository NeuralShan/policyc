DSP-004 Card Transaction Dispute and Chargeback Procedure

1 Purpose
1.1 This procedure describes how customer disputes on card transactions are received, investigated and resolved.

2 Scope
2.1 This procedure applies to disputes raised by retail cardholders on debit and credit card transactions.

3 Intake
3.1 The customer raises a dispute through the mobile app or the contact centre. The Disputes Officer registers the dispute in the case management system.
3.2 Disputes raised more than 120 days after the transaction date are declined.
3.3 For disputes raised within 120 days, the Disputes Officer requests supporting evidence from the customer.

4 Evidence review
4.1 If the evidence is complete, the dispute proceeds to classification.
4.2 If the evidence is incomplete, the Disputes Officer sends a reminder to the customer and reviews the evidence on file again.

5 Classification and resolution
5.1 Disputes of IDR 2,000,000 or less are resolved by the Disputes Officer with a provisional credit to the customer.
5.2 Disputes above IDR 2,000,000 are referred to the Disputes Supervisor.
5.3 Disputes classified as suspected fraud are referred to the Fraud Investigation Unit.
5.4 The Disputes Supervisor either files a chargeback with the card scheme or declines the dispute.
5.5 The Fraud Investigation Unit completes its investigation as described in Annex C.

6 Timelines
6.1 The Disputes Supervisor must decide within 10 working days of referral.
6.2 Chargebacks are filed with the card scheme within 5 working days of the Supervisor's decision.
6.3 Every dispute must be resolved within 14 calendar days of registration.

7 Customer communication
7.1 The customer is informed of the outcome in a timely manner.
