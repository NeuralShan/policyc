LOTO-003 Lockout/Tagout Procedure for Machine Maintenance

1 Purpose
1.1 This procedure protects workers from the unexpected start-up of machinery during servicing and maintenance.

2 Procedure
2.1 The authorized employee notifies all affected employees that the machine will be shut down.
2.2 The machine is shut down using its normal stopping procedure.
2.3 All energy sources (electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic) are isolated.
2.4 The authorized employee applies a personal lock and tag to every energy-isolating device.
2.5 The authorized employee verifies zero energy by attempting to start the machine.
2.6 Maintenance or servicing is performed.
2.7 Verification of zero energy must always be performed before any maintenance or servicing begins.

3 Exceptions
3.1 For minor tool changes expected to take less than 10 minutes, the operator may proceed directly to maintenance after shutting down the machine.

4 Restoring equipment
4.1 After maintenance, the authorized employee removes tools, ensures all employees are clear, and removes their lock and tag.
4.2 Only the employee who applied a lock may remove it.
4.3 Equipment must be returned to service as soon as possible.
