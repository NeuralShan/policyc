EXP-001 Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

1 Purpose
1.1 This policy sets out how employees are reimbursed for business expenses.

2 Scope
2.1 This policy applies to all staff, managers and the Finance Head of the company.

3 Submission
3.1 The requester submits an expense claim with receipts through the expense portal. The Accounts team checks every claim for receipts.
3.2 Claims without receipts are returned to the requester, who must attach the receipts and resubmit; the claim is then checked again.

4 Approval
4.1 Claims below INR 10,000 are approved by the requester's line manager.
4.2 Claims above INR 10,000 require approval from the Finance Head.
4.3 Claims submitted by a manager are approved by the Finance Head.
4.4 The Finance Head's own claims must be approved by the Finance Head.
4.5 Approvers must decide within 3 working days of receiving a claim.

5 Payment
5.1 Approved claims are paid by the Accounts team within 5 working days.
5.2 Every claim must be reimbursed within 7 calendar days of submission.

6 Audit
6.1 Claims escalated by the Audit Committee are re-reviewed by the Internal Audit team before payment.

7 Controls
7.1 No person may approve their own claim.
7.2 Reimbursements must be processed promptly and, where appropriate, in line with company policy.
