IAM-005 User Access Request and Provisioning Procedure

1 Purpose
1.1 This procedure defines how access to business applications is requested, approved, provisioned and reviewed.

2 Scope
2.1 This procedure applies to employees and contractors who require access to any production application.

3 Request
3.1 The requester's line manager raises an access request in the IT service portal, stating the application and the access level (standard or privileged).
3.2 Requests that do not name an application owner are rejected by the Service Desk.

4 Approval
4.1 Standard access requests are approved by the application owner.
4.2 Privileged access requests are approved by the application owner and then by the Information Security Manager.
4.3 Requests for contractors are approved by the Head of Vendor Management.
4.4 Each approver must decide within 2 working days of receiving the request.

5 Provisioning
5.1 After all required approvals, the IAM team creates the account and grants the approved access within 1 working day.
5.2 In an emergency declared by the Duty Manager, the IAM team may grant privileged access immediately, before any approval is obtained.
5.3 No access may be granted before it has been approved by the application owner.
5.4 Access must be provisioned within 3 working days of the request.

6 Review
6.1 Accounts flagged by the quarterly recertification are revoked by the IAM team.

7 Records
7.1 The IAM team retains approval evidence as appropriate.
