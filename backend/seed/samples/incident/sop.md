INC-007 Workplace Incident Reporting and Investigation Procedure

1 Purpose
1.1 This procedure ensures that workplace incidents are reported, investigated and corrected so that they do not recur.

2 Scope
2.1 This procedure applies to all employees, contractors and visitors at the Nagoya plant.

3 Reporting
3.1 Any person who is involved in or witnesses an incident reports it to their supervisor as soon as practicable.
3.2 The supervisor records the incident in the HSE system and classifies it as minor, serious or major.

4 Investigation
4.1 Minor incidents are investigated by the area supervisor.
4.2 Serious incidents are investigated by the Site HSE Manager.
4.3 The investigator identifies the root cause and proposes corrective actions.
4.4 The investigation must be completed within 5 working days of the report.

5 Corrective action
5.1 The department head implements the corrective actions within 10 working days of the investigation.
5.2 The Site HSE Manager verifies that the corrective actions are effective and closes the incident.
5.3 Every incident must be closed within 10 working days of being reported.

6 Independence
6.1 No person may investigate an incident that they reported.
