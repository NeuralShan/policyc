PRC-006 Purchase Requisition and Purchase Order Approval Procedure

1 Purpose
1.1 This procedure sets out how goods and services are requisitioned, approved and ordered.

2 Scope
2.1 This procedure applies to all purchases of goods and services by the Nagoya plant, except utilities.

3 Requisition
3.1 The requester raises a purchase requisition in the ERP system with a cost centre and an estimated value.
3.2 The Procurement team checks whether the item is available from an approved supplier.
3.3 If no approved supplier exists, the requisition is sent for supplier onboarding.

4 Approval limits
4.1 Requisitions up to JPY 500,000 are approved by the Section Manager.
4.2 Requisitions above JPY 1,000,000 are approved by the Plant Director.
4.3 Requisitions above JPY 50,000,000 are approved by the Board of Directors.
4.4 Capital expenditure requisitions of any value are approved by the Plant Director.

5 Purchase order
5.1 Once approved, the Procurement team issues a purchase order to the supplier within 2 working days.
5.2 Requisitions rejected by an approver are closed and the requester is notified.
