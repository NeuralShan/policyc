LVE-008 Annual Leave Request Procedure

1 Purpose
1.1 This procedure describes how employees request and obtain approval for annual leave.

2 Scope
2.1 This procedure applies to all permanent employees.

3 Request
3.1 The employee submits a leave request in the HR portal, stating the start date and the number of working days requested.
3.2 The HR portal checks the employee's leave balance. If the balance is sufficient, the request proceeds to approval.
3.3 If the leave balance is insufficient, the HR portal rejects the request and notifies the employee.

4 Approval
4.1 Requests of 10 working days or fewer are approved or rejected by the employee's line manager.
4.2 Requests of more than 10 working days are approved or rejected by the Head of Department.
4.3 The approver must decide within 2 working days of receiving the request.

5 Outcome
5.1 Approved leave is recorded in the HR portal and the employee is notified.
5.2 Rejected requests are closed and the employee is notified of the reason.
5.3 The employee must be notified of the decision within 3 working days of submitting the request.
