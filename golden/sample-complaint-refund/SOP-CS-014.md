## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines how customer complaints are logged, investigated and resolved, including when a
refund is issued.

## 2. Roles

- **Customer Service Agent (CSA)**: first point of contact; logs and investigates complaints.
- **Team Lead**: supervises CSAs and reviews escalated complaints.
- **Finance Manager**: approves high-value refunds.
- **Service Delivery Lead**: handles complaints the Team Lead cannot resolve.
- **Customer Experience Champion**: reviews closed complaints for recurring issues.

## 3. Service levels

All complaints must be resolved within 5 business days of being logged.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Logging

1. The CSA shall log every complaint in the CRM within 1 business day of receipt, recording the
   order number and the customer's description of the problem.
2. The CSA should verify the customer's identity before discussing account details.

### 4.2 Investigation

3. The team will verify the order details against the order management system within 2 business days.
4. The CSA attaches the Refund Approval Form (RAF) to the complaint record and records whether the
   complaint is upheld.
5. If the complaint is not upheld, the CSA informs the customer of the outcome and closes the
   complaint.

### 4.3 Refund decision

6. If the complaint is upheld and a refund is due:
   - If the refund amount is below ₹5,000, the CSA who logged the complaint approves the refund.
   - If the refund amount is above ₹10,000, the Finance Manager approves the refund within 3 business days.
7. The CSA completes the Refund Approval Form (RAF) with the approved amount.
8. The CSA submits the refund to the payment gateway. If the payment gateway rejects the refund, notify IT Support.
9. The CSA responds to the customer promptly to confirm the refund and closes the complaint.

### 4.4 Disputes and escalation

10. If the customer disputes the resolution, return to step 3.
11. If a complaint is unresolved after 48 hours, escalate it to the Service Delivery Lead.
12. The Service Delivery Lead may send the complaint back to the Team Lead for rework.
